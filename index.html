Philippine Culture

PHILIPPINE CULTURE

Discover the beauty and traditions of the Filipino people!

Tinikling

Tinikling is a famous Filipino folk dance from Leyte. Dancers gracefully move between bamboo poles, showing rhythm, coordination, and Filipino creativity.

Adobo

Adobo is a well-loved Filipino dish cooked with meat, vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and spices. It represents the rich flavors of Filipino cuisine.

Baro't Saya

The Baro't Saya is a traditional Filipino women's outfit. It consists of a blouse and a long skirt, representing Filipino identity, elegance, and cultural heritage.

Bayanihan

Bayanihan is the Filipino tradition of helping one another in a community. It reflects unity, kindness, and cooperation among Filipinos.

TRAVEL TIPS

Make your Philippine adventure fun, safe, and memorable!

Plan Your Trip

Research your destination, check the weather, and prepare your itinerary before traveling.

Respect Local Culture

Learn about Filipino customs, respect local traditions, and be polite to the people you meet.

Bring Essentials

Pack comfortable clothes, sunscreen, water, and other important items for your journey.

Explore Responsibly

Keep destinations clean, avoid littering, and help preserve the Philippines' beautiful natural attractions.
